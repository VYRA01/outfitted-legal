Your closet,
styled by AI.

Photograph what you own. Max, your stylist, sorts it and puts outfits together from it, for the weather, the week and the big days.

Try it early on Android See what it does

Coming to Google Play first, then the App Store.

Meet Max

A stylist who knows every piece you own.

Snap a photo and Max recognises it: top, trousers, dress, outerwear, shoes or accessory, with its colours, pattern and seasons. Then ask him anything.

  • What goes with these trousers? What do I wear to dinner?
  • Outfit ideas built only from clothes you actually have.
  • See what each piece costs per wear, and find the ones you forgot.
Plan ahead

Mornings stop being a decision.

Put outfits on your calendar for the week. Add the days that matter, like a wedding, a birthday or an interview, with the dress code, and Max plans the look around the forecast for that day.

  • Reminders a week before, the evening before and on the morning.
  • Today's outfit on your lock screen, without opening the app.
Share

Post the look. Add a song.

Share a photo or a short video of your outfit, add a song and a place, and follow people whose style you love.

  • Posts are checked before they go up.
  • Report or block anyone, right from their post.
  • Try-ons are labelled as made by AI, never passed off as a photo.
And more

Everything a closet should have done already.

See it on you

Add a full-body photo and Max draws you wearing the look, shoes, cap and bag included.

Dressed for the weather

Today’s forecast at a glance, and an outfit for it: a layer when it’s cold, nothing that suffers in the rain.

Pack for trips

Add a trip and Max packs a bag from your closet for the days away and the weather where you’re going.

Mood folders

Save looks into moods like Cozy, Confident or Date night, or make your own.

On your lock screen

What today is for and the pieces you planned, without unlocking anything.

Made for everyone

English, Polski, Deutsch, Español and Français. Light and dark, larger text, high contrast.

Private by design

Your closet is yours.

Only you can open your closet photos.
They stay private to your account. Posting a look is always your choice.
Location only when you ask.
Your approximate location is used for the forecast if you turn weather on, and to name your town on a post if you choose to.
No ads. No tracking.
And you can delete your account, with everything in it, from inside the app.
