How it works

From a photo to an outfit.

  1. Snap it

    Photograph a piece. Max names it, with its colours, pattern and seasons, and files it in your closet.

  2. Ask Max

    What goes with these trousers, what to wear tonight, what your closet is missing. He only suggests clothes you own.

  3. Plan the week

    Put outfits on the calendar, and mornings stop being a decision. Reminders come a week before, the evening before and on the day.

  4. Dress for the day

    A wedding, an interview, a birthday: add the dress code and Max plans the look around the forecast for that day.

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Email us.

When can I get it?

Android first: a closed test on Google Play is starting now, and you can ask to join it. The iPhone version follows on the App Store.

Is my closet private?

Yes. Your closet photos open only for you. Your approximate location is used only when you ask: for the forecast if you turn weather on, and to name your town on a post if you choose to. No ads, no tracking.

How does Max know what I own?

You photograph each piece once. Max recognises what it is, its colours, pattern and seasons, and even guesses the brand. You can correct anything he gets wrong.

Is a try-on a real photo?

No. Max draws you wearing the look from a full-body photo you add. Try-ons you share carry an “AI try-on” label, so nobody takes them for a photo.

Does it work offline?

Your closet is there, but Max needs a connection: recognising clothes, chatting and drawing try-ons all happen on our server.

Which languages?

English, Polski, Deutsch, Español and Français, with light and dark mode, larger text, a high-contrast look and colours of your own.

Can I delete everything?

Yes, from inside the app: Profile, then Delete account. It removes your account, clothes, outfits, posts and photos for good.
