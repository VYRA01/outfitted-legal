<h2 id="questions">Common questions</h2>

<div class="qa">
<details id="q-delete"><summary>How do I delete my account?<span class="plus" aria-hidden="true"></span></summary><div class="answer"><p>In the app: Profile, then Delete account at the bottom. It permanently removes your account, clothes, outfits, posts and photos.</p>
<p>No longer have the app? Email krzysiu.leszcz@outlook.com from the address on your account and ask for the account to be deleted. We delete it, with everything in it, and write back when it is done, within one month at the latest.</p></div></details>
<details id="q-report"><summary>How do I report a post or block someone?<span class="plus" aria-hidden="true"></span></summary><div class="answer"><p>From the post’s menu, or from their profile. Reports are reviewed, and we aim to act on them within 24 hours.</p></div></details>
<details id="q-reminders"><summary>How do I turn off reminders?<span class="plus" aria-hidden="true"></span></summary><div class="answer"><p>Profile, then the gear (Settings), then Occasion reminders.</p></div></details>
<details id="q-max"><summary>Max isn’t answering, or a try-on won’t finish.<span class="plus" aria-hidden="true"></span></summary><div class="answer"><p>Max runs on our own server, which is occasionally restarted. Wait a minute and try again; a try-on takes anything from twenty seconds to a few minutes to draw. If it keeps failing, email us and say what you were doing.</p></div></details>
<details id="q-look"><summary>Can I change the app’s language or look?<span class="plus" aria-hidden="true"></span></summary><div class="answer"><p>Profile, then the gear (Settings): English, Polski, Deutsch, Español or Français, light or dark, larger text, high contrast and your own colours.</p></div></details>
<details id="q-location"><summary>How do I stop the app using my location?<span class="plus" aria-hidden="true"></span></summary><div class="answer"><p>Location is used for two things, and only when you ask: the weather, if you turned it on, and the name of your town on a post, if you pressed “use where I am”. Turn it off in your phone’s Settings, under the app’s Location permission. A place already on a post stays there until you delete the post.</p></div></details>
<details id="q-data"><summary>Can I get a copy of my data, or have it corrected?<span class="plus" aria-hidden="true"></span></summary><div class="answer"><p>Yes — email krzysiu.leszcz@outlook.com from the address on your account.</p></div></details>
</div>
